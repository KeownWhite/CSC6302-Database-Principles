- Module: 01

- Title: Family Spooky Vacation Database
- Due Date: October 31, 2026
- Points: 100

# Exact Professor Prompt

Design a database schema that keeps track of what you need to pack to go on vacation with your family to your favorite spooky destination.

Consider data like:

- Where does each member of the family want to go?
- What activities could be done in those places?
- What costumes do you need to pack?
- What comfort/safety items do you need to pack?
  - Hats
  - Gloves
  - Scarves
  - Warm wool socks
  - Boots
  - Snacks
  - Hand warmers
  - Glowsticks

The goal is to design a small database and think about how to organize data.

There is no single right answer. This exercise is intended as an early database-design experiment.

# Requirements / Rubric

## 30 Points

List the schema definition for at least three relations.

## 10 Points

Select appropriate types for each column and include them in the schema definitions.

## 60 Points

For each relation:

- List all superkeys.
- List the candidate keys.
- Select the most reasonable primary key.
- Justify the primary-key choice.
